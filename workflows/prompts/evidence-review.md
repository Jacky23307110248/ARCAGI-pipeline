# Evidence reconciliation

Review trajectory claims, independent source findings, and engine probes as separate channels. Source plus a controlled engine probe establish implementation behavior; saved play records establish what was observed; saved notes establish recorded reasoning. Never backfill source-only facts into the trajectory ledger.

For each rule claim choose supported, conflict, or unknown; list supporting and contradicting evidence IDs, scope/version limits, and the next discriminating probe. Keep unresolved or unreachable combinations explicit.
